Scrolling Site Announcement

Write a message that scrolls across the top of the site. It appears on all normal site pages except the homepage, login, register and forgot-password pages. Links remain visible as their full URL and can be opened.

Line breaks are preserved. Any http:// or https:// link becomes clickable. Maximum 2,000 characters.

Your announcement preview will appear here.

Turning this off hides the message without deleting it.
