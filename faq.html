Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using Delight Vendor Logs. If you cannot find the answer you need, contact our support team.

What is Delight Vendor Logs?
Delight Vendor Logs is a digital marketplace where users can explore available digital services, resources, country-number services and other listed offerings.
How do I create an account?
Visit our homepage and select Create Account. Follow the registration instructions and provide the information requested. Keep your login details private.
How do I log in?
Select Login on the homepage and enter the credentials associated with your account. If you cannot access your account, use any available account-recovery option or contact support.
How do I fund my wallet?
Log in to your account and follow the wallet-funding instructions displayed on the platform. Use the payment details provided there and keep your transaction reference in case you need assistance.
What should I do if my wallet balance does not update?
First, check your transaction record and confirm whether the payment was completed. If the balance still has not updated, contact support with your payment reference, amount and approximate payment time. Never share your password, PIN or OTP.
What if my order is pending?
A pending status does not necessarily mean the order has failed. Check the order status again and avoid placing duplicate orders while the original order is being processed. If it remains pending, contact support with the order ID.
What if my wallet was debited but my order failed?
Contact support and provide the order ID and transaction reference. The order and transaction records may need to be reviewed before a correction or eligible wallet credit can be determined.
Can I cancel an order?
Cancellation depends on the service and the order's current status. If a cancellation option is available, follow the instructions shown on the platform. An order that has already been processed may not be cancellable.
How do I request a refund?
Contact support with your order or transaction reference and explain the issue. Requests are reviewed based on the available records, service-provider response and applicable requirements. Read our Refund Policy for further details.
Why is a service unavailable?
Availability may depend on stock, country, provider status, technical conditions or service restrictions. Try again later where appropriate, or contact support if the issue continues.
Where can I get help with my account or purchase?
Use the Contact Support option on the homepage to access the available WhatsApp, Telegram and email support channels. Include your username and relevant order reference, but never send your password or one-time security codes.
How is my personal information handled?
Information may be handled to operate accounts, process transactions, provide services and respond to support requests. Read our Privacy Policy for more information.
Where can I read the platform rules?
Read our Terms and Conditions for the general rules governing use of Delight Vendor Logs.

Still Need Help?

Our support channels are available through the Contact Support option on the Delight Vendor Logs homepage.
