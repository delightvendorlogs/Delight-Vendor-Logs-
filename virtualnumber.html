PORTAL NOTICE

🔐
If your purchased number does not receive a verification code:

Turn on a VPN and connect to the same country as the number you purchased.
🇺🇸
Example: If you purchased a 🇺🇸 USA number, connect your VPN to the United States. You can use either a free or paid VPN.
🔄
If the code still does not arrive: Uninstall and reinstall the app you are trying to register, then try the verification again with the country-matching VPN enabled.
📍
Please make sure: Please make sure your VPN country matches the country of your purchased number.
